I am

Sanika

Interaction Designer

I'm a designer who likes to understand things before figuring out how to make them better. I'm curious about people, their behaviours, and the systems they navigate. I enjoy researching, asking questions, and turning what I learn into interfaces, interactions, visuals, and experiences.

My approach sits somewhere between systems thinking and creative exploration. I like looking at the bigger picture while paying attention to the finer details, understanding why things work the way they do and how they could work better.

I'm working towards becoming a versatile designer who can bring together research, strategy, interaction, and visual design to create thoughtful, meaningful experiences.

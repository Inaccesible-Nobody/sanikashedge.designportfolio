HOW TO ADD TO YOUR PORTFOLIO
1. Copy aqi.html, aqi.css, aqi.js and the aqi-assets folder into your existing projects/ folder.
2. Rename aqi.html to aqi.html if needed (it already has the correct name).
3. Open projects/aqi.html in the browser through your usual local server.
4. The page links back to ../index.html. If your homepage is at a different relative path, update those two links in aqi.html.
